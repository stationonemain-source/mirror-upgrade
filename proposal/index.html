01MU-1

Keep mirrorupgrade.com and its email yours

$500once, then $97 a month
What we found
  • Right now, anyone can send email that claims to be from @mirrorupgrade.com, and nothing tells Gmail or Outlook to refuse it. For a business that takes orders and sends invoices by email, that is the gap fake-invoice scams use. Two records cause it.
  • The domain publishes two SPF records, the list that tells other mail servers who may send as you:
    v=spf1 a mx include:websitewelcome.com include:outlook.com ~all
    v=spf1 a:dispatch-us.ppe-hosted.com include:spf.protection.outlook.com -all
    The rules allow one. A receiving server that finds two treats the check as an error, so neither record counts. The first one still names websitewelcome.com, HostGator's old web hosting.
  • There is no DMARC record. DMARC tells Gmail and Outlook what to do with mail that claims to be from @mirrorupgrade.com but isn't. Without it, a fake invoice from "order@mirrorupgrade.com" has nothing telling the receiver to reject it.
  • The domain, registered since 2010 and now at GoDaddy, comes up for renewal on 19 November. The registry shows a change last November, which looks like last year's renewal, so auto-renew may well be on. Worth two minutes in your GoDaddy account to be sure. If it lapsed, the store and email would stop within days.
  • What is in good shape: the site's certificate is valid to December and renews itself, it sends the right security headers, and the signing keys Microsoft 365 needs (DKIM) are published.

What we do

  • Check the domain renewal and lock it with you on our first call, in your GoDaddy account and your company's name. No charge for that.
  • Replace the two SPF records with one correct record, and publish DMARC: two weeks of reports first, so no real mail gets blocked, then a policy that rejects fakes.
  • Run a full outside security check of your email, website, encryption and DNS (69 checks). We fix what it finds and check again.
  • Every month after: a fresh scan, and an email from us if anything changed.
02MU-2

Put a price on the B304x, and the other 89

$4,000once
What we found
  • All 90 products carry a price of $0.00. Shoppers see no price; Google sees $0.00, which keeps every product out of Google Shopping and breaks the price in your product markup.
  • The Request for Quote page shows the raw code {rfq_content} to visitors, and to Google's crawler.
  • On a phone the homepage took 10.4 seconds to show its main image (Lighthouse, September). The theme dates from 2020.
  • 36 collections exist; the homepage links to 13. Your style and color collections can only be found through the sitemap. No page has a description written for Google (products reuse their spec text), and "Addison" doesn't appear on the homepage or About page.
  • Tapping the email address in the footer opens the phone dialer, because it is coded as a phone link. In September the site said you open at 10 and Google said 9:30.
